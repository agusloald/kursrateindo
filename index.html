All rates are pulled straight from each bank's own official rate page — no third-party aggregator involved. Most banks list RMB as "CNH" (offshore yuan) rather than "CNY" — the scraper reads CNH and shows it here as CNY, since that's what most people search for. None of this is a live feed — each bank updates on its own schedule, so check the date per row before relying on it for a transaction. Always confirm the exact rate with your bank before transacting.
